"The Wonder of teaching is watching caterpillars become butterflies"

Established in 2020, Acumen Junior College is a premier unaided private junior college affiliated with the Telangana State Board of Intermediate Education (TSBIE). Located in the heart of Vidyanagar, Hanumakonda, we offer integrated coaching for IIT-JEE, NEET, Olympiad, BITSAT and EAPCET alongside Intermediate MPC and BiPC streams.

  • check_circle Supportive environment that nurtures ambition and confidence.
  • check_circle Focus on academics, values, and essential life skills.
  • check_circle Modern classrooms, science labs, library, and personalized attention.
  • check_circle Directors and faculty from NIT Warangal, IIT system, and top institutions with proven track records.
  • check_circle Whether you're aiming for JEE, NEET, or overall academic excellence, Acumen Junior College offers the right platform to unlock your potential and build a strong foundation for your future.
